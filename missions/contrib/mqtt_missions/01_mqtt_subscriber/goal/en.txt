Mission goal
============

There is a private meeting that is planned for next week.
To join the meet, you have to get the passphrase.
Fortunately, there is an MQTT topic that broadcasts the passphrase. One of your spies told you that the topic name is GameShell/important_topic
All you have to do is subscribe to that topic and wait for the passphrase.

Useful commands
==============

mosquitto_sub -h localhost -t <topic_name>

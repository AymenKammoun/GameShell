Mission goal
============


You are at your treasure cave. Before entering, the guard has to verify your identity (Authentication).
For that, the guard will give you a random number.
The challenge consists of multiplying the number by 2 and then encrypting the result with your AES key.
Given that the guard has access to your key, he can verify the completion of the challenge.

You can find the challenge random number in challenge.txt in the Cave.

You first have to generate a 128-bit key and IV with respective file names key.bin.hex and iv.bin.hex in the Cave.
Then you have to compute the mathematical operation and encrypt the result in a file named ciphered_result.bin


Useful commands
==============
    Key genearation or IV:
        openssl rand -hex 16 > key.bin.hex
    Encryption: 
        openssl enc -aes-128-cbc -in clear_text.txt -out cipher_text.bin -K $(cat key.bin.hex) -iv $(cat iv.bin.hex)

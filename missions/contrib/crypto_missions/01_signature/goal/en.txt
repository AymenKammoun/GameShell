Mission goal
============

Your friend, King James, has sent you a letter.
However, you have received multiple letters, all claiming to be from King James.
Your task is to identify the genuine letter by verifying the signature using the King's public key.
In the Main Building of the Castle, you will find a Mailbox containing all the unsorted letters.
Your mission is to sort them into either the Genuine or Fake folder based on their signature.

You can find King James's public key in the Library, also located in the Castle's Main Building.

Useful commands
==============

openssl dgst -sha256 -verify public_key.pem -signature signature.bin data.txt
    Verify the signature signature.bin of data.txt using the public key public_key.pem

openssl base64 -d -in signature.sha256 -out signature.256.bin
    Convert a Base64-encoded signature into binary format using OpenSSL
